About us

Investing tools that show their work.

Pear Digitals LLC is an independent software company building clear, transparent tools for retail investors and traders.

Why we exist

Everyday investors deserve the reasoning, not just the answer.

Professional investors work from models: assumptions about growth, margins and what a business should be worth. Retail investors usually get a single price target, or a headline, with no way to see how it was reached or to change it when they see things differently.

We think that should change. Our tools put the numbers and the assumptions in plain view, explain them in plain language, and let you test your own view against them.

What we believe

How we build.

Transparent

Every estimate comes with the assumptions behind it and the data it was built from, so you can check the reasoning yourself.

In your hands

Estimates are estimates. We label them clearly, we don't sell your data, and we don't run ads.
