Privacy Policy — Pear FMV

Effective 11 October 2026 · Published by Pear Digitals LLC

Pear FMV (peardigitals.com/fmv) shows stock prices and fair value estimates, lets you keep a watchlist and your own fair value for a stock, and includes Quill, an assistant for valuation questions. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and who it’s shared with. It covers this app only; our other products have their own policies.

Information you give us

  • Account. Your email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Watchlist. The stocks you add.
  • Your fair values. The instructions you give for valuing a stock (for example “use a 22x multiple”), any reason you add, the resulting values, the method summary and assumptions, previews you haven’t saved yet, and a history of past versions.
  • Waitlists. Whether you joined the Premium or Pro waitlist, which one, and where in the app you joined from.
  • Plan. Whether your account is on the Free or Premium plan.

Quill conversations

When you send Quill a message, the conversation so far is sent to Google’s Gemini API to generate a reply. If you’re signed in, Quill also receives your watchlist and your saved fair values so it can answer questions about them. When you ask for a custom fair value, your instructions are sent to the same service to run the analysis. We don’t store the text of your conversations on our servers: the conversation is kept in your browser tab and is cleared when you close the page or log out. We use Google’s paid Gemini API service; under its terms, Google doesn’t use this text to improve its products, and may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse. Please don’t share personal or sensitive information in chat.

Email alerts

On Premium and Pro, if alerts are on, we check prices for the stocks on your watchlist (daily on Premium, during market hours on Pro) and email you when one reaches your Fair Buy zone. We keep a record of each alert (stock, price, threshold and time) so we don’t send duplicates. Every alert email has a link to turn alerts off, and you can switch them off from the account menu.

Usage records

To enforce daily limits and keep costs in check, we record each Quill message and each fair value analysis: the time, the type of request and the stock involved. For signed-in users these records are linked to your account. For visitors who aren’t signed in, they’re linked to a one-way code derived from your IP address; we don’t store the IP address itself.

Stored in your browser

When you log in, your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay logged in. We don’t use advertising cookies, analytics or cross-site tracking on this app.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: your account, watchlist, fair values and Quill.
  • To keep your fair values up to date: when a stock’s published analysis changes, we re-run your saved instructions automatically.
  • To enforce plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • To email you about your account (for example sign-up confirmation or password reset) and, if you joined a waitlist, to tell you when Premium or Pro is available.

We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase: accounts, database and server functions. Data is stored in Supabase’s Canada (Central) region.
  • Google (Gemini API): generates Quill’s replies and custom fair value analyses.
  • Resend: sends alert emails (your email address and the alert content).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr: serves a software library the page loads.

Like any website, these providers may receive your IP address and basic request information when your browser connects to them. Stock prices and fair value data come from market data providers and our own analysis; none of your personal information is sent to them.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account information, watchlist and fair values (including their history) until you delete them or your account. Resetting a fair value to the published one removes your instructions but keeps the history of past versions. Usage records are kept to enforce limits and monitor costs. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email support@peardigitals.com from the address on your account; deleting the account removes your watchlist, fair values and their history, waitlist entry, plan, alert settings and history, and usage records.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules let each account read only its own records, and sensitive operations run on our servers rather than in your browser. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices and rights

You can browse without an account. You can remove stocks from your watchlist and reset your fair values at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your information; contact us to make a request.

Children

Pear FMV isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Not investment advice

Fair values, Quill’s answers and your custom analyses are estimates for education and research. They aren’t investment advice.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above, and for significant changes we’ll let signed-in users know in the app. Questions: support@peardigitals.com.
