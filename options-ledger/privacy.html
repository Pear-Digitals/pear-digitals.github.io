Privacy Policy — Options Ledger

Effective October 1, 2026 · Pear Digitals LLC

Options Ledger is a manual journal for tracking your options trades. This policy describes what information the app handles and what happens to it. In short: your journal lives on your device, we have no servers that store it, and we do not sell or share it.

Information you provide

Everything in Options Ledger is entered by you by hand: positions, option and stock transactions, premiums, fees, dates, notes, and Quill conversations and drafts. The app does not connect to brokerages, has no market-data feed, and requires no account.

On-device storage and encryption

Your journal is stored in an encrypted database on your device. Encryption keys are kept in your operating system's secure storage (for example, the iOS Keychain). We do not operate servers that hold your journal data and we cannot access it. Protect your device with a passcode and keep its software updated; no storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Quill, the in-app assistant

In the current version, Quill runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Your messages and journal information supplied to Quill are processed on-device and are not sent to Pear Digitals, Apple, or any third party. An option to allow processing through Apple's Private Cloud Compute is planned for a future version but is not active; this policy will be updated if that changes. You can disable Quill in Settings.

Backups you choose to make

You can back up your journal to your own Google Drive from Settings. The backup is encrypted with a recovery phrase that you choose — we cannot decrypt it. Connecting Google Drive uses Google's sign-in, and anything stored there is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy. You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in Settings.

Exports you choose to make

Exporting creates a CSV file that you save or share yourself. Exported files are not encrypted by the app, so only share them with people and services you trust.

No tracking or advertising

Options Ledger has no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The performance statistics shown in the app are calculated on your device from your own entries. We do not sell your information.

Device backups

Your device's own backup service (for example, iCloud Backup) may include the app's data depending on your device settings. Those copies are governed by your platform provider's terms.

Children

Options Ledger is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
