How Pear fair values work

Updated 11 October 2026 · Pear Digitals LLC

A fair value is our estimate of what a stock is worth based on the business, not on where it traded yesterday. Instead of one number, every stock gets three.

Bear, base and bull

  • Bear: what the stock is worth if things go meaningfully worse than expected.
  • Base: our central case, the outcome we think is most likely.
  • Bull: what it’s worth if things go meaningfully better.

Each case comes from its own assumptions about growth, margins and what investors will pay for the business. The three are blended, weighted by how likely we think each one is, into a single fair value that the chart and labels compare against.

How a value is built

We pick the method that fits the company rather than forcing one formula on every stock. Depending on the business that can be earnings or cash-flow multiples, a discounted cash flow, or valuing segments separately. Inputs come from company filings with the SEC (quarterly and annual reports, results releases) and market data.

Analyses are written with the help of AI, working from those sources. Custom fair values and Quill’s answers are AI-generated too, so they can contain mistakes.

Fair Buy and Strong Buy

  • Fair Buy: the price is at or below fair value.
  • Strong Buy: the price is far enough below fair value to leave a wide margin of safety.
  • % above FMV: how far the price sits above fair value.

When values change

Fair values are re-rated after events that change the outlook for the business:

  • Earnings and other results releases
  • Guidance changes
  • Stock splits, mergers, spinoffs and major deals
  • Significant regulatory decisions
  • Investor days

A price move on its own doesn’t change a fair value. Every company reports quarterly, so each value is revisited at least once a quarter. The chart keeps the history of past values so you can see what changed and when.

Your own fair value

If you see a stock differently, ask Quill to change it, for example “use a 22x multiple” or “assume slower growth.” Your version is built from the same analysis with your changes applied, is marked Custom, and updates after the same events. You can reset to ours at any time.
