Our mission

Democratize options trading.

Options have long been treated as a specialist's game, wrapped in jargon and scattered across spreadsheets. We believe anyone willing to learn should be able to understand their trades and keep a clear record of them.

Pear Digitals builds software that makes options simple to follow: honest numbers, plain language, and your data kept in your hands.

Accessible

Plain-language tools designed for people at every level of experience.

Transparent

You see exactly what was recorded and how results are calculated.

Private

Your journal lives on your device. No ads, no selling your data.
